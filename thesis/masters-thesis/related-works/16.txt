Cross Container Attacks: The Bewildered eBPF on Clouds

Conducted real-world attacks using only valid eBPF programs (no kernel bugs exploited)


They created a special tool using eBPF, called a snoop tool, that helps attackers find important processes (like cron, bash, or sshd) running outside the container, but in a VM.

They showed how to hijack those processes and escape the container

They ran these attacks on real cloud services and found many of them were vulnerable

EVALUATION

They tested 11 real online coding platforms, 5 of them could be attacked.

They tested 4 major cloud providers, 3 had vulnerable Kubernetes clusters.

They found that about 2.5% of Docker containers had settings that allow eBPF attacks.
