Vulnerability Analysis of 2500 Docker Hub Images

Provide an up-to-date snapshot of container security across different image types

Docker Hub contains over 3.5 million images, but many may be outdated or vulnerable


IMPLEMENTATION:

Analyzed 2,500 images from Docker Hub

Used open-source vulnerability scanners called Clair.

EVALUATION

82% of certified images had at least one high/critical vulnerability.

Official images were the most secure. 45.9% had high/critical vulnerabilities.

Community images had the highest average vulnerabilities

Only 17.8% of images had no vulnerabilities.


Houdini can be used to test these images based on the CVEs given.
